Set up Intraday

This app reads US stock market prices from a company called Alpaca. To do that it needs two long passwords from your own free Alpaca account — a “key ID” and a “secret key”. They only read price data; they cannot move money, and nothing here ever places an order. They are stored in a file on this Mac that only you can read, and are never sent anywhere else.

How to get them (about three minutes)

  1. Open app.alpaca.markets and sign up (free).
  2. Make sure the switch at the top left says Paper, not Live.
  3. On the right, find “API Keys” and click Generate. Copy both values before closing that box — Alpaca shows the secret only once.

Nothing you type here is written to the screen, the logs, or the internet apart from the single check with Alpaca that the keys work.
